Ireland's corporation tax in 2025
€32.9 billion

Ireland collected €32.9 billion in corporation tax in 2025, 17% more than in 2024. This excludes €1.7 billion from the Apple State Aid case; with it, the total was €34.7 billion.

Reading the numbers

What the terms on this page mean

Tax charged in the accounts and tax paid in cash

Company accounts show tax in two different ways, and the two can differ a lot in any one year.

Tax charged in the accountsThe tax that belongs to this year's profit, whether the group pays it now or in later years. It is the figure used to work out a group's tax rate.
Tax paid in cashThe money that actually left the group for tax authorities during the year. It can include tax on earlier years' profits, tax paid in advance and instalments of older bills, minus any refunds.

Pfizer is an example. Its tax charge for 2025 was a credit of $0.3bn, because of tax it expects to save in later years, but it paid $4.7bn in cash. The Ireland chart uses tax paid in cash, because that is what groups now report by country.

Pre-tax profit

What a group earned after its costs, before income tax. Groups split it between profit made by their US companies and profit made by their companies abroad.

Where profit is reported

A multinational is a group of many companies. Profit is counted in the country of the company that records it, which often depends on which company owns patents or other rights. Sales are counted where the customers are.

The 5% rule

For financial years starting in mid-December 2024 or later, US accounting rules (known as ASU 2023-09) require groups to name any country that receives 5% or more of the income tax they paid in cash. Countries below that line are grouped together as "other".

What "US" includes

For most groups, US tax is federal tax plus state and local tax, which their reports show on separate lines. A few groups report it differently, and each group's page says which applies.

A US loss

Some groups report a loss in the United States while making a large profit abroad. The group as a whole is still profitable. In the profit chart, this is marked with an arrow.

Not yet covered

Groups whose financial year started before mid-December 2024 did not yet have to name countries. They will in their next annual report.

About this page

Sources, method and updates

All figures come from each group's latest annual report (Form 10-K) filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. Figures on this page are rounded, to the nearest $0.1bn, $1m or $0.1m depending on size, and percentages to the nearest whole number. Exact amounts are in each filing, linked for every group. We took them from the income tax note and the note on sales by country. Amounts are in US dollars. Each group's page links to the tables we used.

Which groups are included. We cover large US pharma, biotech, medtech and tech multinationals, focusing on groups with large intangible assets and operations in Ireland. We do not include data-centre operators or consulting firms. Medtronic is included although it is not a US company on paper: in 2015 it moved its legal home to Ireland when it bought Covidien, a move known as a corporate inversion. It is still run from Minneapolis and files US annual reports.

Who is not in the 100, and why. Some large groups with big operations in Ireland are not on this page. TikTok, X and Stripe are privately owned and file no annual report (Form 10-K) with the US Securities and Exchange Commission, so there is no 10-K to draw on. TikTok and X do have Irish companies that file accounts with the Irish Companies Registration Office (CRO), which is where their Irish tax can be found. Groups headquartered outside the US, such as European pharma groups, are outside the set, because this page covers US multinationals and the US rule on naming countries applies only to groups that file US annual reports. Some groups are in the 100 but do not name countries yet. Analog Devices, Broadcom, HP and Synopsys, among others, are not yet under the rule, because their financial years began before mid-December 2024; they will name countries in their next annual reports. Hologic was taken private by Blackstone and TPG in April 2026, so its report for the year to September 2025 is its last.

What "US" includes. For most groups, US tax paid is federal plus state and local tax, shown on separate lines in their reports. There are seven exceptions:

  • Eli Lilly shows only US federal tax. The $0.9bn left after federal tax and Ireland is not broken down and may include US state tax; we show it as "other".
  • AbbVie shows US federal and foreign tax only, with no separate line for state tax.
  • Amgen and Medtronic show a single line for all US tax.
  • DocuSign and Pure Storage (now Everpure) show US state tax only, with no federal line.
  • IQVIA shows no US line at all; any US tax is inside "other".

Which groups operate in Ireland. A group that does not name Ireland may still pay tax there. We checked each such group's list of subsidiaries (Exhibit 21 of its annual report) for Irish companies and, where that list leaves out smaller subsidiaries, other public sources such as IDA Ireland, company websites and the Irish company register. Four groups showed no Irish operations: Netflix, Nvidia, Micron and Texas Instruments. We show them separately rather than as paying Ireland under 5%. Each group's page names the Irish company or site we found and links to the source.

Comparing with Irish tax receipts. We compare the total paid to Ireland with Ireland's corporation tax receipts for 2025, €32.9 billion excluding the one-off payment from the Apple State Aid case (Department of Finance).

We will update this page as new annual reports come out. Eight groups are not yet covered by the rule on naming countries: Adobe, Agilent, Analog Devices, Becton Dickinson, Broadcom, HP, Hewlett Packard Enterprise and Synopsys. Their first reports under it follow between November 2026 and January 2027. Hologic, now privately owned, will not file another report. 2026 reports from the groups whose years end in December follow from February 2027, and 2026 reports from the groups whose years end in December follow from February 2027.
